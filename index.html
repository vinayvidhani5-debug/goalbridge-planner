Assumptions, Limitations & Educational Disclaimer

Educational Simulation Only: This application is built as an educational scenario-planning tool for GoalBridge workshops. It does not recommend financial products, guarantee investment returns, or provide regulated investment advice.

Mathematical Conventions: Goal costs are inflated annually. Monthly contributions are calculated under an Ordinary Annuity model compounding monthly at nominal rate $r/12$. Taxes, expense ratios, and sequence-of-returns volatility are not modeled. No personal data is stored or transmitted.
